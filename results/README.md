# Results

## Course experiment results

`standardized_models_results.xlsx` contains 28 result tables covering Linear
Regression, XGBoost, LSTM and N-BEATS across calendar, temporal, clustering,
input-window and zero-handling configurations.

The lowest weighted sMAPE recorded in the workbook is:

| Workbook sheet | Model | Features | Input window | Clusters | Weighted sMAPE |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `both_features_xgboost` | XGBoost | Calendar and temporal | 84 days | 1 | **27.8442%** |

This is the best recorded result from the original Applications of AI course
experiments.

## Additional time-ordered benchmark

`time_safe_benchmark.csv` is generated by `src/time_safe_benchmark.py`. This
post-course benchmark uses three expanding-window validation folds and reserves
the final 15% of dates for one final evaluation.

| Model | Final-period sMAPE | WAPE | MAE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| XGBoost, zero-handled | **42.60%** | **32.12%** | **15,438.71** |
| XGBoost, raw active period | 42.94% | 32.33% | 15,539.38 |
| Naive lag-1, raw active period | 56.40% | 52.11% | 25,045.70 |

These benchmark results are supplementary and are not part of the original
course result submission.
